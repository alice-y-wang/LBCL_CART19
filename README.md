# Large B Cell Lymphoma CART19 Dataset 
This repository contains the code used to generate figures and perform analysis for the manuscript titled "Opposing roles of TNFa and interferon signaling in CART19 therapy response in large B-cell lymphoma". 

---
**Abstract**
Chimeric Antigen receptor T cell targeting CD19 (CART19) achieves durable remission in 30% of large B-cell lymphoma (LBCL) patients. The mechanism through which circulating myeloid cells contribute to CART efficacy is unknown. We performed longitudinal (apheresis to 1 month post-CART19) CITE-seq and T-cell receptor sequencing of 363,265 peripheral blood mononuclear cells from 15 LBCL patients, including 65,310 CAR+ T cells, together with spatial proteomic profiling of >6 million cells from 10 matched pretreatment tumors. Remissions longer than one year were associated with post-infusion TNF–NF-κB signaling in CAR+ effector-memory T cells and increased LTA/LTB–TNFR signaling between CAR+ T and myeloid cells. Progressive disease was instead characterized by persistent type I and II interferon programs across CAR+ T and myeloid compartments, accompanied by inflammatory CCL4, IL1B, and TNFSF10 signaling. Spatial analyses revealed concordant myeloid–T-cell relationships in tumors. These findings define coordinated response-associated immune systems and identify chronic interferon signaling as a potential therapeutic vulnerability in CAR T-cell resistance.
